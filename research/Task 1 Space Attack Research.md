## Official Space Attack Instructions

[Official user manual](https://archive.org/details/SpaceAttackforEmersonArcadia2001/Space_Attack_Emerson_Arcadia_2001_Manual/page/n3/mode/2up) from internet archive.
```
## WHAT THE GAME IS ALL ABOUT 

1. The goal is to score as many points as possible. 

2. Points are gained by shooting down the invading enemy space 
ships with your missiles. 

3. The enemy space ships will fire rockets back at your missile 
launchers which you must avoid. Watch out for enemy space 
ships that will try to crash into your missile launcher. 

4. On the bottom/left side of the screen is located a fuel gauge 
to indicate the fuel of the missile in combat. 

When the gauge is indicating empty, a warning signal is heard. 

If the missile launcher continues fighting when the gauge 
indicates empty, the missile launcher explodes and the next 
one continues the combat. 

5. The number that is located on the bottom right side of the 
screen indicate the group of attackers, after group 1 is eliminated 
the second group of attacker appears on the screen and the 
number change to 2. The firing from the enemy space ships will 
be more and more intensive as the group number becomes greater.
```

## YouTube Description
[Gameplay footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jYIC8ADIArc)
```
Space Attack is a space shooter game released in 1982 for the Emerson Arcadia 2001 console by UA Ltd./Emerson Radio Corp. It's a clone of Namco's 1979 arcade hit "Galaxian."

Players control a ship at the bottom of the screen, using three missile launchers to defend against waves of attacking alien spacecraft. The objective is to destroy all enemies in each armada while using evasive maneuvers to avoid incoming fire.

The title adds strategic elements beyond basic shooting. Players must monitor their fuel gauge, as running out of fuel ends the game. Successfully clearing all enemies brings a faster, more challenging wave. Skilled players who reach 5,000 points earn a bonus missile launcher
```
